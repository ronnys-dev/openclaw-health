OpenClaw Health — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Who uses this app

OpenClaw Health is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses only the owner's own Google account.

What data is accessed

With the owner's consent, the app requests read-only access to the following Google Health API data: sleep, activity and fitness (workouts, steps), and health metrics (resting heart rate, heart rate variability). The app never writes, modifies or deletes any health data.

How data is used

Data is used solely to show the owner summaries and analysis of their own sleep, training and recovery through their personal assistant. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's private server with access restricted to a single system user.
  • Health data is not sold, shared with or transferred to any third party.
  • To produce summaries, relevant data may be sent to the AI model provider chosen by the owner, under that provider's terms.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
